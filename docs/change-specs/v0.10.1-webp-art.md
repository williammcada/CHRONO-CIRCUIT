**Status:** Owner-approved 16 October 2026 as Phase 2 of the cross-project game-loading plan (Math-Arcade `docs/change-specs/game-loading-2026-10-16.md`): WebP quality 90 at the same size, released after the owner approves a side-by-side comparison; PNG originals kept in `art-source/`. ## Change

- `title.png` (2.16 MB) and `foundry.png` (1.90 MB), both 1536×1024 painted backgrounds, are served as `title.webp` (258 KB) and `foundry.webp` (220 KB). Originals moved to `art-source/`.
- References updated in `game.js`, `style.css` and `scripts/render-game-qa.mjs`.
- `scripts/build.mjs` includes `.webp` in the offline cache list, so the installed offline copy still has both backgrounds.
- Version 0.10.1-rc.1 (page title, stylesheet query, `BUILD`, package).

## Not changed

Pixel-art sprite atlases and the seven stage environments (70–120 KB each, read byte-for-byte by the Tempo animation tests), gameplay, saves, math, music.
