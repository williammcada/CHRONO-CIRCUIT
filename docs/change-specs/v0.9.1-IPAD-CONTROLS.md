# v0.9.1 — iPad viewport recovery and cross-shaped D-pad

User-authorized maintenance change, 20 September 2026. Baseline: main `ba22e640af82296bf29fc8ee40f82c4a01abf237` (v0.9.0).

## Scope

- Prevent transient keyboard/visual-viewport offsets from being retained as the game frame's position. Recalculate after focus loss, overlay dismissal, viewport resize/scroll, rotation and return to the page. Keep form panels scrollable and never force-scroll while a form control has focus.
- Place sharp world labels in the same local coordinate system as the game canvas.
- Arrange touch directions in a conventional cross: up above center, left/right on the middle row, down below center. Preserve action buttons, simultaneous presses, keyboard/gamepad mappings and pointer release/cancellation.
- Reserve sufficient rail height for three rows of at least 44px targets and bottom safe-area padding; preserve the complete 16:9 image above controls.
- Preserve all math, save data, progress, adult settings, gameplay, audio and art. No reset-game or soundtrack feature is included.
